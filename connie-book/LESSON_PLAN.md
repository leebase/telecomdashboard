# Connie Book 12-Week Lesson Plan

## Guiding Notes

- This plan assumes a sustainable pace of 4-6 focused hours each week.
- Every week advances both writing skill and AI-tool fluency.
- Deliverables should be "good enough to keep momentum," not polished to
  exhaustion.
- The book category stays open through the discovery phase.

## Weekly Plan

| Week | Writing Goal | AI Goal | Primary Deliverable | Secondary Exercise | Time Budget | Portfolio Artifact |
|------|--------------|---------|---------------------|--------------------|-------------|--------------------|
| 1 | Capture raw story material, lived experience, and possible themes | Learn basic prompting, note capture, and transcript cleanup | A 2-page story inventory or life-experience map | Record a 10-minute voice note and turn it into bullet points with AI help | 4-5 hours | Story inventory and cleaned transcript excerpt |
| 2 | Identify likely book directions without committing too early | Learn comparison prompts, clustering prompts, and summary prompts | A short memo listing 3 possible book directions | Ask AI to compare the 3 directions by audience, emotional pull, and effort | 4-5 hours | Book-direction decision memo |
| 3 | Define the reader and the problem, promise, or emotional payoff | Learn audience-framing and persona prompts | A one-page ideal-reader profile | Rewrite the reader profile for two alternate audiences and compare clarity | 4-6 hours | Reader profile and prompt set |
| 4 | Build a rough working outline with 8-12 possible sections | Learn outline generation and critique workflows | A working outline with section notes | Use AI to stress-test the order and identify weak sections | 4-6 hours | Outline v1 plus revision notes |
| 5 | Draft one short opening section in a natural voice | Learn voice-preservation prompts and light revision workflows | A 700-1000 word opening section | Ask AI for 3 revision passes: clarity, structure, and specificity | 4-6 hours | Draft with tracked revision notes |
| 6 | Draft a second section from memory or lived experience | Learn summarization and expansion loops | A second 700-1000 word section | Convert voice notes into rough prose, then refine by hand | 4-6 hours | Before-and-after draft example |
| 7 | Practice simple research integration without losing voice | Learn source-note extraction and fact-check support | A research-backed section or scene memo | Use AI to summarize 2-3 sources into clean note cards | 4-6 hours | Source-note sheet |
| 8 | Revise earlier material into a stronger mini-chapter | Learn critique prompts, gap-finding, and rewrite planning | One revised mini-chapter or combined section | Ask AI to identify repetition, missing transitions, and vague claims | 4-6 hours | Revision checklist and improved sample |
| 9 | Shape book material into professional portfolio evidence | Learn transformation workflows: draft to summary, draft to pitch, draft to excerpt | One polished excerpt and one short project summary | Turn a chapter fragment into a one-page sample for future opportunities | 4-5 hours | Polished excerpt |
| 10 | Document the writing system and the AI workflow used to support it | Learn process documentation and prompt library curation | A simple "how I work" guide | Save 5-10 prompts that were genuinely useful and annotate them | 4-5 hours | Prompt library v1 |
| 11 | Review the full quarter and find the strongest direction | Learn retrospective and prioritization prompts | A quarter review memo: what worked, what to keep, what to drop | Ask AI to compare effort vs payoff across the first 10 weeks | 4-5 hours | Quarter review memo |
| 12 | Prepare the next 90-day plan for writing and employability | Learn planning prompts and backlog design | A next-quarter plan with 3 realistic priorities | Turn the next-quarter plan into issue-like backlog items | 4-6 hours | Next-quarter roadmap and backlog draft |

## Completion Standard

Each week is considered complete when:

- the primary deliverable exists in draft form
- the secondary exercise is attempted, even if imperfectly
- one reusable artifact is saved
- at least one note is added about what made the work easier or harder

## AI Tool Categories To Practice

- brainstorming and discovery
- transcript cleanup
- comparison and clustering
- outlining and structural critique
- revision and clarity passes
- summarization and note extraction
- retrospective and planning support

## Human Guardrails

- AI can accelerate reflection, structure, and revision, but Connie keeps final
  ownership of voice and truth.
- Stop using a tool when it adds friction or flattens the writing.
- Preserve raw notes before any AI transformation so the original material is
  never lost.
