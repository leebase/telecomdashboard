# Connie Book Project Brief

## Mission

Build a daughter-first learning system that helps Connie write a book, learn AI
tools while writing it, and accumulate credible work habits and portfolio
signals that position her well ahead of entry level for a future return to the
workforce.

This workspace also serves as a practical sandbox for learning
[`openai/symphony`](https://github.com/openai/symphony) by defining a small
cooperative agent team, a repo-owned workflow contract, and an hourly planning
loop that advances one backlog item at a time.

## Primary Audience

- Connie as the primary learner, writer, and eventual operator
- Lee as sponsor, editor, and accountability partner
- The agent team as project support infrastructure

## Desired Outcomes

1. Produce a practical 12-week lesson plan that combines book-writing progress
   with AI-tool skill development.
2. Establish a sustainable weekly writing and learning rhythm that fits life
   with two children under 3.
3. Build a reusable project system: roadmap, backlog, workflow contract, agent
   missions, and run log.
4. Generate portfolio evidence of AI-enabled work: prompts, process notes,
   research summaries, outlines, revisions, and operating artifacts.
5. Learn Symphony-style orchestration through real project management instead of
   a detached demo.

## Constraints

- Pace must remain light and sustainable: target 4-6 focused hours per week.
- The work is private-first by default.
- The book type is intentionally undecided at bootstrap.
- Early wins should reduce overwhelm, not create a second full-time job.
- The first phase is planning and curriculum creation, not publishing or app
  development.
- Agent activity is limited to `connie-book/` unless a later backlog item
  expands scope deliberately.

## Success Metrics

- `LESSON_PLAN.md` contains a complete 12-week curriculum with both writing and
  AI-learning objectives.
- `ROADMAP.md` describes a coherent 90-day path with milestones and weekly
  cadence.
- `BACKLOG.md` contains prioritized work items with clear owners and definitions
  of done.
- `WORKFLOW.md` is compatible with Symphony's repo-owned workflow model and can
  guide an isolated agent run.
- `RUN_LOG.md` contains at least one end-to-end example of backlog selection,
  agent collaboration, and proof of work.
- Connie can point to at least three portfolio-style artifacts by the end of
  the 12-week cycle.

## Non-Goals

- Shipping a public-facing product in phase 1
- Publishing the book before the material and writing habit are ready
- Allowing unsupervised agents to modify the rest of the repository
- Depending on Linear integration before the local workflow is stable
- Optimizing for flashy automation over sustainable human progress

## Operating Principles

- Daughter-first beats demo-first.
- One meaningful backlog item per run is enough.
- Human review is preferred over hidden autonomy.
- Reusable artifacts matter as much as raw output.
- The agent team exists to lower friction and increase clarity.

## Canonical Artifacts

- `PROJECT_BRIEF.md`: purpose, constraints, and definition of success
- `ROADMAP.md`: 90-day operating plan
- `LESSON_PLAN.md`: 12-week curriculum
- `BACKLOG.md`: issue-like work items
- `WORKFLOW.md`: Symphony-compatible execution contract
- `agents/`: role definitions and handoff rules
- `RUN_LOG.md`: hourly loop history and proof of work
